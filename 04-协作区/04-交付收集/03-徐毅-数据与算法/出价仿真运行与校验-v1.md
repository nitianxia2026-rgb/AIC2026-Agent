## 5. 人工手算 1: CPM 场景

**场景**: 餐饮美食 × 信息流广告，日预算 500 元，k=1.2，基准情景

**公式**:
- est_cpm = base_cpm × k
- share(k) = k^α / (k^α + β)
- rd = share(k) / share(1)
- baseline_imp = B / base_cpm × 1000
- traffic = baseline × rd
- affordable = B / est_cpm × 1000
- sim_imp = min(traffic, affordable)
- clicks = sim_imp × CTR
- conv = clicks × CVR
- spend = min(B, sim_imp × est_cpm / 1000)

**代入值**: base_cpm=17.75, base_ctr=0.01631, base_cvr=0.02098, k=1.2, α=1.2, β=1.5, B=500

**中间量**:
- est_cpm = 21.30
- share(1.2) = 0.4565, share(1) = 0.4000
- rd = 1.1413
- baseline_imp = 28169.02
- traffic = 32148.76, affordable = 23474.65
- sim_imp = min(32148.76, 23474.65) = **23474.65**
- clicks = 23474.65 × 0.01631 = **382.87**
- conv = 382.87 × 0.02098 = **8.0327**
- spend = min(500, 23474.65 × 21.30 / 1000) = **500.00**

**脚本结果**: imp=23474.65, clicks=382.87, conv=8.0327, spend=500.00 → **一致 ✅**
